Safety First

Do not try to DIY hazardous electrical work

Do not assume a smart switch is interchangeable with any conventional switch. Compatibility, box space, wiring, neutral requirements, load type, and manufacturer instructions can matter.

For electrical faults, panel work, new circuits, high-load equipment, wet-location electrical work, or concealed wiring, a qualified electrical professional can evaluate the system and determine the appropriate next step.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about smart home electrical

Do smart switches always need a neutral wire?

Not every device has the same requirement. The specific switch, wiring configuration, load, and manufacturer instructions determine compatibility.

Can you wire security cameras?

Electrical work may include power and wiring support for security equipment, depending on the system and project scope.

Can smart lighting be added to an older home?

It may be possible, but the existing wiring, boxes, switches, circuits, and device requirements should be evaluated first.
