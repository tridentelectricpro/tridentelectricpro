Safety First

Do not try to DIY hazardous electrical work

Electric heaters can draw substantial current. Do not use extension cords or undersized circuits for permanent heater loads, and do not bypass overcurrent protection to keep a heater running.

For electrical faults, panel work, new circuits, high-load equipment, wet-location electrical work, or concealed wiring, a qualified electrical professional can evaluate the system and determine the appropriate next step.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about baseboard & wall heater electrical

Do baseboard heaters need dedicated circuits?

Many electric heating installations use dedicated circuits, but the correct arrangement depends on the equipment, load, and electrical system.

Why does a heater trip the breaker?

Possible causes include overload, a circuit issue, wiring problems, or equipment faults. Repeated trips should be diagnosed rather than bypassed.

Can you add heater wiring during an addition?

Yes. Heater circuits can be included in the electrical plan for an addition when the equipment and project requirements are known.
