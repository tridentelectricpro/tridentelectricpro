Common Electrical Problems

What brings a homeowner or business owner to an electrician?

Electrical calls often start with a symptom: a breaker that trips, an outlet that stops working, lights that flicker, a circuit that loses power, wiring that needs to be changed, or a new appliance or equipment installation that requires electrical capacity.

Other projects are planned rather than emergency-driven. These include panel upgrades, whole-home rewiring, new lighting, EV charging, generator systems, home additions, kitchen and bathroom remodels, new homes, commercial improvements and multifamily electrical work.

Breaker trips or power loss Flickering lights Dead or damaged outlets Old or inadequate wiring Panel capacity concerns Lighting upgrades Home EV charging Backup power
Electrical Safety

Do not try to diagnose or repair hazardous electrical work yourself

Electrical systems can involve energized conductors, service equipment, fault current and other hazards. If you see burning, sparking, smoke, repeated breaker trips, damaged wiring or another potentially dangerous condition, avoid opening equipment or attempting a repair yourself.

A qualified electrical professional can assess the situation using appropriate test equipment, protective equipment and safe work procedures.

Bellevue Climate & Electrical Considerations

How local weather can affect electrical planning

Western Washington has a mild marine climate with a wetter cool season and a generally drier summer pattern. Seattle-area National Weather Service climate information shows substantially more precipitation in the cooler months than in July and August. See National Weather Service information.

Wet-season conditions

Rain and moisture make outdoor electrical equipment, exterior lighting, receptacles and other exposed systems important considerations. Electrical work should account for the equipment's intended location and protection requirements.

Winter outages and backup power

Homeowners and businesses may consider generator and backup-power planning where continuity of power matters. See generator services.

Summer electrical projects

Longer, drier summer periods can be convenient for planned outdoor lighting, EV charging, remodel and other electrical upgrades, subject to project requirements.
