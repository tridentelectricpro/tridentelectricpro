Common Reasons for Electrical Service

Problems and projects customers may need help with

Repairs & troubleshooting

Breaker trips, dead circuits, flickering lights, failed outlets, damaged wiring and other electrical symptoms can require professional diagnosis.

Planned upgrades

Panel upgrades, dedicated circuits, lighting changes, EV chargers and backup power can require electrical planning and installation.

Construction & remodeling

New homes, additions and remodeling can involve new circuits, rewiring, panels, outlets, switches, lighting and equipment connections.

Weather Context

New York weather and electrical planning

New York has a humid continental to humid subtropical climate. In broad terms, local conditions include cold winters in many areas, warm summers and strong regional variation. Actual conditions vary by city and elevation, so this is planning context rather than a forecast.

Weather exposure can matter for outdoor lighting, exterior receptacles, equipment, generators and other electrical systems. Seasonal heating or cooling loads can also affect electrical demand. Any specific electrical installation should be evaluated for its property, equipment and applicable requirements.

Electrical Safety

Do not turn a small electrical symptom into a larger hazard

Electrical work can involve shock, arc-flash and fire hazards. Do not open energized equipment, bypass protective devices or attempt repairs when you do not know what is energized.

For repairs, installations and upgrades, use an appropriately qualified electrical professional with suitable test equipment, protective equipment and safe work procedures.

Call instead of DIY when you see

  • Sparking or arcing
  • Burning smell or scorching
  • Repeated breaker trips
  • Damaged or exposed wiring
  • Unexpected loss of power
  • Electrical work needed for construction or remodeling
