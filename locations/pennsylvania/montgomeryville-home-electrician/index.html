Local Weather

Pennsylvania weather and electrical planning

Pennsylvania has a humid continental climate. In general, local conditions include cold winters, warm summers and varied terrain and precipitation. Because Montgomeryville conditions can differ from the rest of the state, use this as planning context rather than a forecast.

Outdoor electrical work

Weather exposure matters for exterior lighting, outdoor receptacles, equipment connections and other electrical systems installed outside. The correct equipment and installation method depend on the specific location and application.

Seasonal electrical demand

Heating, cooling, portable equipment, pumps and other seasonal loads can change how much electrical capacity a property uses. Planned additions should be evaluated as part of the overall electrical system.

Storm-related concerns

After severe weather, customers may notice outages, damaged equipment, tripped breakers or other electrical symptoms. Do not handle damaged energized equipment yourself; contact an appropriate professional.

Electrical Safety

Why DIY electrical repairs can be dangerous

Electrical work can expose a person to shock, arc-flash, fire and other hazards. A problem that looks like a simple outlet or breaker issue can involve wiring, connections or equipment elsewhere in the circuit.

Do not remove covers, work inside energized equipment, bypass protective devices or attempt a repair when you are unsure what is energized. A qualified electrical professional should use appropriate test equipment, protective equipment and safe work practices for the task.

When to stop and call

  • Burning smell or visible scorching
  • Sparking or arcing
  • Repeated breaker trips
  • Damaged or exposed wiring
  • Unexpected power loss in part of a property
  • Electrical work needed for a remodel or addition
