Rhode Island has a regional climate. In broad terms, local conditions include weather conditions vary considerably by season and local geography. Actual conditions vary by city and elevation, so this is planning context rather than a forecast.

Weather exposure can matter for outdoor lighting, exterior receptacles, equipment, generators and other electrical systems. Seasonal heating or cooling loads can also affect electrical demand. Any specific electrical installation should be evaluated for its property, equipment and applicable requirements.

Electrical Safety

Do not turn a small electrical symptom into a larger hazard

Electrical work can involve shock, arc-flash and fire hazards. Do not open energized equipment, bypass protective devices or attempt repairs when you do not know what is energized.

For repairs, installations and upgrades, use an appropriately qualified electrical professional with suitable test equipment, protective equipment and safe work procedures.

Call instead of DIY when you see

  • Sparking or arcing
  • Burning smell or scorching
  • Repeated breaker trips
  • Damaged or exposed wiring
  • Unexpected loss of power
  • Electrical work needed for construction or remodeling
