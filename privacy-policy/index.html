1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Trident Electric ("Trident Electric," "we," "us," or "our"), headquartered at 999 110th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98004, collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit tridentelectricpro.com (the "Website"), contact us, or request our electrical services anywhere in the United States.

By using the Website, you acknowledge this Policy. It does not apply to third-party websites, apps or services that the Website links to.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: name, phone number, email address and preferred contact method.
  • Service request details: property type, service needed, urgency, project description, street address, city, ZIP code, preferred date and time, and how you heard about us.
  • Communications: the content of emails, calls, texts and messages you send us.

Please do not send passwords, Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or health information through the Website.

Information collected automatically

When you use the Website, our hosting and technology providers may automatically receive technical data such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring page and date and time of visits. See Section 5 on cookies.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries and schedule, quote, perform and follow up on electrical services.
  • Verify requests, prevent spam and fraud, and protect the security of the Website and our business.
  • Communicate about your service, including confirmations and service updates.
  • Send marketing communications where permitted by law and, where required, with your consent.
  • Maintain business records, improve the Website and our services, and comply with legal, licensing, tax and insurance obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We disclose personal information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run the Website and our business, such as form-processing (Formspree), email, website hosting, scheduling, telephone and accounting providers. They may use the information only to perform services for us.
  • Legal and safety: to comply with law, subpoenas or government requests, to enforce our rights, or to protect the safety of people or property.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, sale or reorganization, subject to this Policy.
  • With your direction or consent.

5. Cookies, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

The Website and our service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Website working, remember settings, protect against abuse and understand how the Website is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended.

We honor opt-out preference signals such as Global Privacy Control where required by law. Because we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising, a signal does not change how we handle your information. There is no uniform standard for "Do Not Track" browser signals, and the Website does not respond to them differently.

6. Calls, text messages and email

If you provide your phone number, we may call or text you about your request or service. We send marketing text messages only with your prior express consent where required by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). Consent to marketing messages is not a condition of purchasing any service. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts or HELP for help.

Our marketing emails comply with the CAN-SPAM Act and include an unsubscribe option. You will still receive messages about services you requested.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this Policy, including to complete your request, keep business, tax and licensing records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections for the Website. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights. We extend them to all U.S. residents who contact us, whether or not a particular state law applies to our business.

  • Know and access the personal information we hold about you, and the categories of sources and recipients.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions (such as required business records).
  • Opt out of sale, sharing for targeted advertising, or profiling. We do none of these.
  • Limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect it for purposes that would require this right.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not deny services or charge different prices because you exercised a right.

How to make a request

Email tridentelectricpro@gmail.com, call (855) 599-0507, or write to Trident Electric, Attn: Privacy, 999 110th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98004. We may ask for details to verify your identity, such as the name, phone number or address on your request. An authorized agent may submit a request for you with your written permission.

We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days, and may extend by another 45 days where permitted, with notice to you. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision or contacting us with the subject line "Privacy Appeal." We will respond to appeals within 60 days, and if still unsatisfied you may contact your state attorney general.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
