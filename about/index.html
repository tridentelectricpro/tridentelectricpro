About Trident Electric: common questions

Who founded Trident Electric?

Jennifer Diana founded Trident Electric in Bellevue, Washington, in 2011.

Is Trident Electric licensed?

Yes. We hold electrical licenses in all states. Our headquarters license is Washington State Electrical Contractor License TRIDEEL741D4 (UBI 606135927), issued by the Department of Labor & Industries. Verify it on the L&I website.

Do you serve areas outside Washington?

Yes. We are licensed in all states and provide service throughout the USA. Browse our service areas to find your state and city.

Do you serve homes and businesses?

Yes. We provide residential, commercial and multifamily electrical services, including new construction, additions and remodeling.

Where are you located and when are you open?

Our headquarters is at 999 110th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA 98004. Business hours are Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
