Contact questions

What are your business hours?

We are open Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

What should I include in my request?

Share the type of property, the service you need, your city and ZIP code, and a short description of the problem or project. Mention any breaker trips, flickering, outages or equipment involved.

What if it is an emergency?

Call 911 for fire, smoke or any danger to people. For other urgent electrical problems, call (855) 599-0507 instead of using the form, since a phone call is the fastest way to reach us.

Do you work on residential and commercial properties?

Yes. We provide residential and commercial electrical services. Select your property type in the form so we can route your request correctly.

Can I request a specific date and time?

Yes. Add a preferred date and time window in the form. We will confirm availability when we contact you.

How do you use the information I submit?

We use it to respond to your request. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
